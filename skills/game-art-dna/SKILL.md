# game-art-dna

## Description and trigger
Use when defining reusable visual rules for a profile or consumer project.

## When to use
Capture style keywords, palette, shape language, and consistency rules.

## When NOT to use
Do not store secrets, copyrighted source files, or opaque prompt histories as Art DNA.

## Conceptual dependencies
Profile, art director, references, and provenance.

## Execution
Keep the document compact, versioned, and reviewable by humans.

## Outputs
`art-dna.json` suitable for planner and auditor inputs.

## Limits and care
Art DNA guides generation; it does not guarantee visual similarity or legal clearance.
